# Assumptions

Small choices made without asking, so that work could continue. Documents cite these as
`[A-nnn]`. A capability's edge cases that no source states are one assumption for the whole
capability, not one per line. At every gate the operator sees how many new ones there are and the
ten that matter most; approving the gate accepts the phase's assumptions, and any of them can be
promoted to a question (it then gets a `D-` record and this row points to it).
